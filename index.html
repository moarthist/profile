About

The eye of a historian,
the voice of a translator.

I believe objects only speak when someone tells their story well. That is where research becomes something a public can share.

I'm Mahmoud, an art historian and translator based in Paris. I studied English Translation at the University of Isfahan and Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna, with Erasmus+ stays in Liège and Paris.

Today I work on Persian visual culture — from Sasanian texts to printed textiles — and on how museums and galleries mediate it, most recently as an art mediator in Romainville and at the Grand 8 Festival in Paris.
