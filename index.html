My experience

An art historian and translator trained in Iran, Italy, Belgium and France — from a bachelor's in English Translation to a master's in Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna.

Recently an art mediator in Romainville and at the Grand 8 Festival in Paris.

My philosophy

I value close reading, clear description and careful staging. An object only speaks when someone tells its story well — that is the work of the museum.

My lifestyle

I play the setar, a traditional Iranian instrument, and photograph nature, architecture and art history. It's not just a hobby — it's a way of seeing.
