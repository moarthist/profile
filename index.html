About

I work where art history, translation and the museum meet — how objects are read, described and staged for a public.

After a Bachelor's degree in English Translation, I pursued a Master's in Iranian Art History at the Isfahan University of Art, where I conducted an in-depth transtextual study of the Kalamkari textiles of India and Iran. I then completed a Master's in Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna, focusing on virtual exhibitions and museums, and in September 2026 began a Master's in Oriental Studies at Sorbonne Nouvelle University in Paris.

My research explores how digital technology can make cultural heritage more accessible, better preserved and more engaging — and how exhibition design can turn historical material into a story people want to follow.
