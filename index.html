Working principles

Three rules I work by.

01

Read closely.

An object only speaks when someone has looked at it long enough to listen.

02

Describe clearly.

Good labels and good translations share one goal: nothing lost between languages.

03

Stage with care.

Space, light and sequence decide whether a visitor follows a story or walks past it.

About

I translate between objects, languages and the people who meet them.

I studied English Translation at the University of Isfahan, then Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna, with Erasmus+ stays in Liège and Paris. Somewhere between those cities, translation and art history became one practice.

Today I work on Persian visual culture — from Sasanian texts to printed textiles — and on how museums and galleries mediate it, most recently as an art mediator in Romainville and at the Grand 8 Festival in Paris.

Away from the desk I play the setar, a traditional Iranian instrument, and photograph nature, architecture and art history.
