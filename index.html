About

I studied English Translation at the University of Isfahan, then Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna, with Erasmus+ stays in Liège and Paris.

I work on Persian visual culture — from Sasanian texts to printed textiles — and on how museums and galleries present it, most recently as an art mediator in Romainville and at the Grand 8 Festival in Paris.

Away from the desk I play the setar, a traditional Iranian instrument, and photograph nature, architecture and art history.
