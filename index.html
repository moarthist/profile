Info

I'm Mahmoud Mohammadi, an art historian based in Paris with a background in translation, Iranian art history and museology — studied and practised across Iran, Italy, Belgium and France.

After a Bachelor's in English Translation and a Master's in Iranian Art History — with a transtextual study of the Kalamkari textiles of India and Iran — I completed a Master's in Visual Arts, Museology and Curatorship at the University of Bologna, focusing on virtual exhibitions and museums.

Focus

    Museology

    Collections, read closely.

    How museums collect, describe and share objects — with a focus on virtual exhibitions and digital access to heritage.

  2. 002

    Scenography

    Space as narrative.

    Arranging rooms, light and sequence so visitors move through a story rather than past a list of objects.

  3. 003

    Exhibition Design

    From research to wall.

    Turning art-historical research into labels, layouts and printed matter that a public can follow.

  4. 004

    Between five languages.

    Persian, English, French, Italian and Arabic — carrying texts, ideas and audiences across cultures.
